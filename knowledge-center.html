❓ Frequently Asked Questions

You can change or reset your Active Directory credentials instantly in Service Catalog → IT Department → Password Reset, or use the ⌘K search bar and query "Password reset".
Submit a letter request form in **Service Catalog → Human Resources → Service Letter Request**. The letters are signed, stamped and uploaded to your Profile page within 2 business days.
IT tickets resolution time depends on priority:
• Critical (Major service offline) — 2 hours.
• High (Productivity block) — 4 hours.
• Medium (Standard ticket) — 24 hours.
• Low (Minor improvements) — 3 days.
Please raise a general support ticket under HR Services or search the Employee Directory for the Finance/Payroll managers to start a direct Lexi chat.
